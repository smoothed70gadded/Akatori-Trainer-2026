**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Akatori**. This trainer provides optional assistance during Akatori's exploration and combat. It supports Mako's core gameplay systems, including staff-based attacks, health management, movement abilities, and progression through dangerous amber-corrupted areas.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Akatori focuses on momentum-based platforming. Mako uses jumping, double jumps, gliding, dashes, wall movement, slides, staff hooks, and environmental objects such as bamboo launch points to cross gaps and reach vertical areas. Enhanced Movement is most useful during difficult traversal sections, while normal movement should be restored for precision platforming.

### Tools & Inventory

Collect amber crystals while exploring and use the game's normal progression and purchase systems. Keep the trainer's Unlimited Amber option disabled while managing ordinary resource collection, then enable it when additional supplies or supported upgrades are needed.

### World & Environment

The game features interconnected amber-corrupted worlds with hidden routes, environmental hazards, collectibles, and ability-gated passages. New staff techniques open previously inaccessible areas, making backtracking an important part of exploration. Use Enhanced Movement to reach difficult platforms and Unlock Staff Abilities to access supported traversal routes.

### Combat & Weapons

Mako fights with a versatile staff that functions as both a weapon and a traversal tool. Combine ground and aerial attacks with movement, dodging, gliding, and staff techniques to handle more than twenty enemy types and several bosses. Unlimited Health and God Mode are suited to learning attack patterns, while One-Hit Defeat enables faster clearing.
